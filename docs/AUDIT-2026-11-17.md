# Solent Marine Outboards UK — Technical, SEO, AI-Visibility & Keyword Audit

**Date:** 17 Nov 2026 · **Site:** https://solentmarineoutboards.co.uk · **Status:** REVIEW ONLY — nothing has been built or changed.
**Sources:** codebase (`src/`, `public/`, `docs/`), live crawl of all 149 sitemap URLs, live headers/robots/agent files, Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, Semrush (browser UI), PageSpeed Insights.
**Method:** run in batches; each batch was re-checked against a second source before being kept (see "Double-check log" at the end).

---

## 1. Verdict in one paragraph

The site is **technically healthy but commercially invisible**. Speed, accessibility, canonicals, titles, H1s, redirects and agent files are all in good shape (PageSpeed mobile 100/100/100/100). The poor performance comes from four things that are *not* technical bugs: (1) a brand-new domain with effectively zero authority (Semrush Authority Score 0, 2 referring domains, 8 junk backlinks), (2) Google has **not crawled two-thirds of the site** (101 of ~150 URLs "Discovered – currently not indexed"), (3) the pages that are indexed are **thin and templated** (product pages ~190 words with no description copy, blog posts 143–438 words against a planned 700–3,000), and (4) a **keyword strategy aimed at high-volume head terms** that a zero-authority site cannot win, with pages mapped to the wrong URLs and visible cannibalisation. There are also **trust/compliance risks** (review statistics, unverifiable brand claims, inconsistent address) that should be resolved before any further promotion.

## 3. Root causes of poor performance (ranked by impact)

### RC1 — Google isn't crawling the site (indexation), driven by zero authority + thin pages
- 101 pages sit in "Discovered – currently not indexed": Google knows the URLs from the sitemap but hasn't spent crawl budget on them. This is the classic pattern for a new domain with no backlinks and low-value/templated pages.
- Even the strategic pillar posts (`/blog/outboard-horsepower-guide/`, `/blog/electric-outboard-motors-explained/`, `/blog/affordable-outboard-motors-uk/`) and `/brands/` are in this bucket — the pages the keyword plan relies on most are not in the index.
- Bing, by contrast, has indexed 117 URLs but shows almost no impressions, which confirms the problem is **ranking power**, not discoverability.

### RC2 — Content is too thin to compete (and templated)
| Page type | Count | Live words (main content) | Problem |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product pages | 108 | 189–201 (all near-identical length) | **No description copy rendered.** Page is a spec table + review form. Several rows show "N/A" (Cylinder volume, Cylinder matrix, Throttle RPM range, Charging alternator). Spec labels are awkward ("Horsepower Out", "Dry Sump Weight", "Official Brand"). Meta description is a formula and in places mis-formats price (`£745.2`). |
| Blog posts | 20 | **143–438** (plan: 700–3,000) | Most posts are 170–290 words — below the threshold where Google will index/rank them for informational queries. 3 H2s each. |
| Category pages | 5 | 618–749 (mostly product cards) | One-line intro, **zero H2s**, no unique buying-guide copy. |
| Brand pages | 6 | 394–730 | Same: one-line intro + cards, zero H2s. Honda (394) and Tohatsu (443) are the thinnest. |
| About | 1 | 182 | Far below the >700-word entity page the build standard expects. |
| Homepage | 1 | 1,024 | Only 4 H2s; **no links to any blog post**; 8 product links. |

### RC3 — Keyword strategy: right data, wrong match to the site's strength
- The keyword map targets the *biggest* terms (e.g. "outboard motors for sale", "electric outboard motor" 17.8K cluster, brand terms) with a domain at Authority 0. Actual Google rankings for those are position **50–90** (pages 5–9). Realistic first wins are long-tail / low-KD terms (KD ≤10, volume 50–300) where thin-authority sites can reach page 1.
- The one thing getting real impression volume is **"diesel outboard"** (≈290 impressions across ~50 queries; the diesel blog post has 1,037 impressions) — a page about a product the shop doesn't sell. It generates impressions but no sales intent.
- Per-product mapping is essentially empty ("114 products inherit category keywords"), so 108 product pages have no distinct search target and ~190 words of copy.
- Homepage H1 ("UK's Premier **Outboard Engine** Specialists") does not contain the mapped primary ("outboard motors for sale"), and category H1s ("Portable Outboards (2.5HP – 6HP)") don't contain their mapped primaries ("5hp outboard engine", "boat motor 10hp", "electric outboard motor").

### RC4 — Link authority is nil
- 2–8 backlinks total, none relevant (no marine, dealer, manufacturer, forum, Isle of Wight / Solent directory, or press links). Bing says the same.

## 4. Keyword mapping & cannibalisation audit

### 4.1 Evidence of wrong-URL ranking (Semrush UK, positions 40–100)
| Keyword (volume) | Mapped to | Actually ranking URL(s) | Issue |
|---|---|---|---|
| boat motor 10hp (320) | `/shop/mid-range/` | **`/shop/`** | `/shop/` steals the category's primary |
| 10hp outboard (320) | `/shop/mid-range/` | `/shop/mid-range/` **and** `/shop/` (3 rows, alternating) | Google can't pick one → classic cannibalisation |
| outboardmotor (480) | — | `/shop/` ×2 | Unassigned, hub catching it |
| small outboard motors for sale (260), cheap outboard motors (110), boat motors small (140) | blog / portable | `/shop/` | Hub outranking intended pages; no category page owns "small/cheap" |
| diesel outboard (210) | blog post | blog post **and** `/blog/` index | Index page competing with the post |
| dinghy outboard motor (140) | blog pillar | `/blog/` index | Pillar not in index (Discovered, not crawled) |
| inflatable kayak engine / kayak outboard | blog pillar | blog pillar | OK, but position 47–60 |

### 4.2 Structural overlaps in the plan itself
- **`/shop/` vs `/shop/{brand}/` vs `/shop/{category}/` vs `/brands/`**: four layers all showing the same 108 products with a one-line intro and near-identical templates. Brand and category pages overlap heavily (e.g. a Yamaha 60hp is on `/shop/`, `/shop/high-horsepower/`, `/shop/yamaha/`). Without unique copy per page, Google treats them as near-duplicates.
- **`/brands/` hub vs `/shop/{brand}/`**: `/brands/` was mapped to brand-navigational queries, but each brand page also targets the brand terms.
- **Blog vs category:** "Best outboard for tender/kayak/inflatable" (pillar #3), "Small outboard motors buying guide" (#4) and `/shop/portable/` all chase "small outboard / dinghy / inflatable" intent. Pillar #1 (electric explained) vs `/shop/electric/` overlap on "electric outboard".
- **Blog accessory cluster (stands, covers, locks, trolleys, brackets, fuel tanks)**: each post is ~150–200 words and links to `/shop/parts/`, but the parts catalogue is only 14 products and has few of those accessories, so the posts promise things the shop can't sell.
- **Excluded/avoided intents are right** (used, "near me" servicing, competitor brands) — keep.

### 4.3 Keyword-map problems
- Map refers to **114 products**; live site has **108** (6 removed) — update.
- Brand-hub primaries for Tohatsu/ePropulsion have no qualifying keyword; these pages (394–687 words, no H2) are low value as separate indexable URLs.
- Primary keywords for blog posts are mostly volumes of 20–390 yet the plan ties them to cluster volumes of 1,000–17,000 — overstated expectations.
- No page owns the highest-intent *long-tail* commercial queries that actually appear in GSC (e.g. "suzuki df140bg price", "mercury pro xs 115 price", "40 hp electric outboard motor price", "yamaha 2.5 outboard", "5hp outboard for sale", "outboard clearance sale"). Product pages are the natural owners but have no copy/price-intent structure.
- AI-prompt-style queries appearing in GSC ("best outboard engine brands for a RIB in the UK, ranked highest first?") are likely tool-generated; I would exclude them from keyword decisions.

## 9. Recommended plan (NOT started — for your approval)

### Phase 0 — Decide & verify (no code)

3. **Blog:** bring each post to the originally planned depth or prune; merge the six accessory posts into one genuinely useful "outboard accessories" guide unless the shop actually stocks them; add author/date/`dateModified`, sources.
4. **About page:** real, verifiable story (>700 words), team, address, premises, warranty/dealer proof.

### Phase 3 — Re-map keywords for a low-authority site
- Re-run mapping with **KD ≤ 15, volume 50–500** as primary targets; one URL per intent (assign "boat motor 10hp / 10hp outboard" to `/shop/mid-range/` only and de-optimise `/shop/` for those terms); give `/shop/` its own distinct "marine motors for sale / stock directory" job.
- Target product-model long-tail (price/“for sale”/model + HP queries already seen in GSC).
- Fix H1/title alignment (homepage + categories carry their mapped primaries).
- Decide the future of the "diesel outboard" post: keep as an awareness page but add a clear route to petrol/electric alternatives (it's earning the impressions).

### Phase 4 — Authority & AI visibility (off-site)
Google Business Profile (if premises are real), Bing Places, manufacturer dealer-locator listings (Yamaha/Suzuki/Honda/Mercury/Tohatsu), UK marine directories, Solent/Isle of Wight local listings, marine forums, a few genuine customer reviews on Trustpilot/Google, local press. Then re-measure AI mentions in Semrush.

### Phase 5 — Measure
Re-check GSC indexing (target: >120 indexed within 4–6 weeks), impressions at positions 1–20, merchant listings, Bing citations.
